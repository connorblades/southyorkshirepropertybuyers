The bits of South Yorkshire that fall between the towns

Plenty of South Yorkshire property sits in places that do not obviously belong to one town. The Dearne Valley runs across the Barnsley, Rotherham and Doncaster boundaries, so a house in Bolton-upon-Dearne or Goldthorpe can be hard to place. Mexborough sits in Doncaster but reads as Rotherham to most people. Penistone is Barnsley on paper and Peak District in feel. We buy in all of it. If you are not sure which town page applies to you, it does not matter, the offer process is the same.

How it works

  1. Tell us about the property. Fill in the short form or ring us. We need the address and a rough idea of the condition.
  2. We ring you back. As fast as we can. You will speak to someone who can make you an offer there and then, not a call handler.
  3. Written offer the same day. A real figure with the basis it rests on, valid for 14 days. No obligation.
  4. You choose the completion date. Usually 7 to 28 days. We use SRA-regulated solicitors, and you can instruct your own at any stage.
