Why houses in north Nottinghamshire often need a cash buyer

A lot of good houses here are hard to sell on the open market, and mining is usually the reason. The Nottinghamshire coalfield ran through Worksop, Warsop, Clipstone and Ollerton, and the housing built for the pits is still here. Some of it isn't standard brick and block, which many lenders won't lend on. Old workings under the ground mean a coal authority search, and that can hold a sale up or stop it. Those are the houses we tend to buy.
