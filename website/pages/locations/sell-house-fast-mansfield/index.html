Why some Mansfield houses are hard to sell

Some Mansfield houses are far harder to sell through an agent than the average figures suggest. It usually comes down to mortgage lenders. If a lender won't lend on your house, most buyers walk away. Here are the five reasons that happens most often round here.

Coal mining searches

Nearly all of Mansfield sits inside the Development High Risk Area for the Nottinghamshire coalfield, so a coal mining search is part of almost every sale here.

Mine gas and contaminated land

Old mines still give off methane and carbon dioxide, known here as stythe. A surveyor may flag mine gas and ask for more tests before a mortgage goes ahead.

PRC and defective stock

If your house is concrete-built rather than brick, most lenders won't touch it. The council holds a list covering Wates, Cornish, Airey, Reema, Hawksley, Orlit, BISF and Trusteel.

River Maun flood risk

Houses near the River Maun can be hard to insure, and buyers walk away when they find out. Flood Re only covers homes built up to 1 January 2009.

How to verify any Mansfield cash buyer, including us

The cash-buying industry has people in it whom you wouldn't want to deal with. The Property Ombudsman, BBC consumer investigations and Action Fraud all describe a recurring set of predatory practices: price-chipping after survey, completion-day gazundering, FCA-unauthorised sale-and-rent-back, fake proof-of-funds, pseudo-local landing pages. Nottinghamshire Trading Standards has specifically prosecuted Mansfield-area property and home-improvement scams: Simon Horsfield of Kirkland Avenue, Mansfield was sentenced to five years in 2018 for a £414,000 East Midlands fraud; a separate ring of six fraudsters received 22.5-year aggregate sentences for a £10.5m doorstep coatings fraud running 2014-2018. A short, sensible check filters bad operators out. None of it takes more than fifteen minutes.

  1. Companies House. Search at find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk.
  2. Proof of funds. A solicitor's letter on letterhead, dated within 14 days, confirming cleared funds in client account. Screenshots of bank balances aren't the same.
  3. FCA register (only if sale-and-rent-back is on the table). Any buyer offering to buy your home and let you stay on as a tenant must be FCA-authorised under MCOB 4.11 and 6.9, check register.fca.org.uk before signing anything. We don't offer sale-and-rent-back.
  4. ICO data protection registration. Every UK company processing personal data must be registered with the Information Commissioner's Office.
  5. Independent reviews. Honest acknowledgement: SYPB's public review density is still building. The other checks here matter more.
  6. Your right to your own solicitor. Always available. A legitimate buyer supports this and never pressures you toward only their nominated solicitor. The Property Ombudsman has flagged the "panel solicitor" model as a structural consumer harm. Citizens Advice North Nottinghamshire (Mansfield Civic Centre, NG19 7BH, 0300 561 2800), the SRA's solicitors register, and any local Mansfield conveyancer can give you a free second opinion. None has any reason to recommend us.

What a legitimate buyer will never ask for: an upfront fee from you; a deposit from you; a signature on anything binding before they have made you a written offer; an insistence that you use only their solicitor as a condition of the sale; or a price drop on completion day without a clearly evidenced material change from a written survey.
