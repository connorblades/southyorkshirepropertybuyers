Flood risk is what stops most sales here

Flood risk is the biggest reason a west Lincolnshire sale falls through. Gainsborough and the parishes along the Trent sit across Environment Agency Flood Zones 1, 2 and 3. The flood search comes back weeks into the sale, the buyer's lender gets cold feet, and the whole thing collapses. We look at flood risk when we make the offer, not later. That's usually why people in DN21 ring us.
