A few things you may be wondering.

What kinds of homes do you buy?

We consider terraces, semis, well-kept homes and properties needing work. Tell us the condition, tenure and timing, and we will explain whether the home is a fit.

How will you assess my property?

We consider the property, its condition and any legal issues, then explain a written offer and its conditions. Ask us about anything unclear before deciding. There is no obligation to accept.

Are there fees or any obligation?

There is no charge to ask for an offer and no obligation to accept it. We charge no estate agent fees. We can cover legal fees if you use our panel solicitor; you can choose your own solicitor and pay their fees instead.

Can the offer change?

We base our offer on the property and the information available. It may change if legal checks reveal a material problem or the property differs from its description. We explain the offer and conditions in writing, and you can ask your solicitor to review them.
