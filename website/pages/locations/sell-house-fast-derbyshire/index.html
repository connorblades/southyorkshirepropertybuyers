Sheffield-facing Derbyshire

Much of north east Derbyshire looks to Sheffield rather than Derby. Dronfield, Eckington and Killamarsh have Sheffield postcodes, and most people there travel into Sheffield for work. That is why we cover them. Further south and east, Bolsover and Shirebrook have the same ex-colliery housing and mining ground as the Nottinghamshire and South Yorkshire coalfield. Lenders are often wary of it, which can make a normal sale hard.

How it works

  1. Tell us about the property. Fill in the short form or ring us. We need the address and a rough idea of the condition.
  2. We ring you back. As fast as we can. You will speak to someone who can make you an offer there and then, not a call handler.
