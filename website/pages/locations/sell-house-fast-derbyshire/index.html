Sheffield-facing Derbyshire

A large part of north east Derbyshire looks to Sheffield rather than Derby. Dronfield, Eckington and Killamarsh carry Sheffield postcodes, sit inside the S catchment, and trade on Sheffield commuting. That is why we cover them. Further south and east, Bolsover and Shirebrook carry the same ex-colliery stock and mining ground issues as the Nottinghamshire and South Yorkshire coalfield, with the same effect on mortgage lending.

How it works

  1. Tell us about the property. Fill in the short form or ring us. We need the address and a rough idea of the condition.
  2. We ring you back. As fast as we can. You will speak to someone who can make you an offer there and then, not a call handler.
  3. Written offer the same day. A real figure with the basis it rests on, valid for 14 days. No obligation.

Frequently asked questions

Which parts of Derbyshire do you buy in?

The north east of the county: Chesterfield Borough, North East Derbyshire District and Bolsover District. We work to roughly a 20 mile radius of our South Yorkshire base, so we do not usually buy in the Peak District or south Derbyshire.

Do you buy in Dronfield and Killamarsh?

Yes. Both carry Sheffield S postcodes and sit inside our core catchment despite being in Derbyshire.

Do you buy ex-colliery property in Bolsover and Shirebrook?

Yes. Mining searches, non-traditional construction and lender caution are the norm across that stock, and they are the situations we are set up for.

How fast can you complete in Derbyshire?

Typically 7 to 28 days from acceptance, on a date you choose.

Nearby counties we cover

We work to roughly a 20 mile radius of our South Yorkshire base, which takes us across four county boundaries: South Yorkshire · Nottinghamshire · Lincolnshire.
