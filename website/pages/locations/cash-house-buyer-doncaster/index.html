Six checks every Doncaster seller should make before accepting a cash offer

Run these on us. Run them on any other cash buyer you speak to. They're all free and they take about half an hour. They are the difference between a clean sale and one you regret.

1. Companies House registration. Search the company name and number at find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk. It should say the company is active, not "in administration" or "dissolution proposed". Check the address matches what the buyer told you, that the owners are named, and that the accounts are up to date.

2. Proof of funds, on a solicitor's letterhead. Ask the buyer's solicitor for a letter confirming the money is there and ready. It should be dated within the last 14 days and show the solicitor's SRA number. A screenshot of a bank statement isn't good enough, because anyone can fake one. A solicitor's letter puts their insurance on the line.

3. Check the solicitor is real. Take the name and SRA number from that letter and look them up on the Law Society's Find a Solicitor directory. It takes half a minute. If the firm or the person isn't listed, stop there.

4. Look them up yourself. Search the company name with the words "review", "scam" and "complaint". Read the reviews on Trustpilot and Google directly, not the screenshots on the buyer's own website. Watch for a brand new review profile, a rush of five-star reviews that all sound alike, or "team" photos that turn out to be stock images.

5. Get your right to your own solicitor in writing. This is the most important check of the six. The SRA Code of Conduct (section 6.2) stops one firm acting for both sides when their interests clash, and in a house sale they always do. Your solicitor is there to protect you, not the buyer. If a buyer insists you use their solicitor, or won't confirm your right to choose in writing, walk away.

6. Get a free second opinion. Citizens Advice Doncaster runs a housing advice service by appointment at Priory Place, Doncaster DN1 1TT. Call 0808 278 7891. It's free and independent. They won't recommend a particular buyer, but they will tell you what a proper sale looks like and point out anything odd in your contract. Our post on how to spot a legitimate cash buyer goes into the warning signs.

The FCA sale-and-rent-back warning every Doncaster seller needs to know

If a cash buyer offers to buy your house and let you stay in it as a tenant, stop. This is called "sale-and-rent-back". It has been regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority since 30 June 2010. Any firm offering it must be FCA authorised and listed on the FCA register at register.fca.org.uk. Check there first.

The FCA imposed effective moratoria on new SRB business through 2010 to 2012 after thousands of consumers lost their homes following firm failures, evictions, and rents being raised to unaffordable levels. As at 2026, the regulated SRB market is effectively dormant. We aren't aware of any UK firm currently offering new SRB agreements to consumers under FCA authorisation. That means if anyone, particularly someone targeting Doncaster repossession-route sellers, offers to "buy your house and let you stay", they are very likely operating illegally under section 19 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (the general prohibition on carrying out regulated activities without authorisation). Section 23 makes this a criminal offence with up to two years' imprisonment.

We don't offer sale-and-rent-back. We aren't FCA authorised for it and we won't be. If you're facing repossession in Doncaster, read our facing repossession page or the can't afford mortgage guide. Both set out the legal routes open to you, including selling to pay the mortgage off in full.
