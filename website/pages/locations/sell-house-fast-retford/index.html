How is the Retford property market right now?

Houses in Retford are selling, but slowly, and prices are only creeping up. A typical Retford house waits around 16 weeks before a buyer is even agreed, and most sell for a little under the asking price. The average Bassetlaw home was worth about £198,000 in February 2026, according to the HM Land Registry UK House Price Index. Retford itself sits well above that average, mostly because of the London train. It's worth looking at recent Retford sold prices before you judge any offer.

How long that takes depends on which part of DN22 you're in:

  • DN22 6 (Babworth, West Retford): about four months to find a buyer. Fewest sellers give up.
  • DN22 7 (Hallcroft, Bracken Lane) and DN22 5 (town centre): about four months, with a few more sellers taking the house back off the market.
  • DN22 4 (Ordsall, and the ex-council houses): closer to five months.
  • The villages (DN22 8 Tuxford, DN22 9 Sturton-le-Steeple, Sutton-cum-Lound): five to six months, the slowest in the area. Around one seller in five gives up.

The wider picture is steady. Interest rates have settled after coming down from their peak. The bigger change, if you're a landlord, is the Renters' Rights Act 2025, which came in on 1 May 2026. It scrapped Section 21. To get your property back so you can sell, you now use Ground 1A: four months' notice, and no re-letting for 12 months if you change your mind. A lot of small landlords in DN22 4 and DN22 5 are getting out because of it.

Retford has a Doncaster postcode but a Nottinghamshire council. Your DN22 postcode comes from the Doncaster postal district. Your council is Bassetlaw District Council. It bills your council tax, sets the empty-homes premium and handles planning and conservation areas. If you're facing repossession, your case goes to Mansfield County Court, not Doncaster. National house-buying websites often get this wrong.

What are Retford's distinctive sale challenges?

Plenty of Retford houses are harder to sell through an agent than the market figures suggest. The usual reason is the mortgage. Lenders won't lend on some homes here without conditions attached, so buyers walk away rather than deal with it. We buy a lot of those houses. There are four common reasons a Retford sale sticks.

The commuter premium

Retford station sits on the East Coast Mainline, with direct trains to London in about 90 minutes. That's why DN22 6 and DN22 7 cost more than nearby Worksop.

River Idle flood risk

The River Idle runs through Retford, and parts of town sit in Flood Zone 2 or 3. In a flood zone a sale often falls apart at survey.

The empty-homes clock

An empty Retford house costs double council tax once it has stood empty 12 months. On a Band D house that adds about £2,237 a year before other bills.

Selling with a tenant

You can sell with the tenant still living there and avoid the eviction process. Since Section 21 went you use Ground 1A, which means four months' notice.

The River Idle joins the River Trent at West Stockwith near Misterton, and LNER runs from Retford to London King's Cross. The empty-homes premium uses powers in the Levelling Up and Regeneration Act 2023, and Retford sits on the East Midlands side of Bassetlaw. Flood zones come from the Environment Agency flood risk map.

How to verify any Retford cash buyer, including us

The cash-buying industry has people in it whom you wouldn't want to deal with. The Property Ombudsman, BBC consumer investigations and Action Fraud all describe a recurring set of predatory practices: price-chipping after survey, completion-day gazundering, FCA-unauthorised sale-and-rent-back, fake proof-of-funds, pseudo-local landing pages. A short, sensible check filters these out. None of it takes more than fifteen minutes.

  1. Companies House. Search at find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk.
  2. HMRC anti-money-laundering supervised business register. Any cash property buyer must appear on the HMRC AML supervised register. Absence is a hard red flag.
  3. ICO data protection registration. Every UK company processing personal data must be registered with the Information Commissioner's Office.
  4. Proof of funds. A solicitor's letter on letterhead, dated within 14 days, confirming cleared funds in client account. Screenshots of bank balances aren't the same.
  5. SRA solicitor cross-check. Whichever solicitor the buyer says is acting for them, verify on the SRA's Find a Solicitor tool. Any "solicitor" not on the SRA register isn't a solicitor.
  6. Your right to your own solicitor. Always available. A legitimate buyer supports this and never pressures you toward only their nominated solicitor. Bassetlaw Citizens Advice (Worksop and Retford offices) and any local Retford conveyancer can give you a free second opinion.
