Make the property one manageable part of planning care.

Arranging care can involve several people and decisions that do not all move at the same pace. You may be helping a relative, managing a home that is now empty or trying to understand whether a sale is appropriate. You do not need to have every practical detail settled before asking about the property.

We can consider the home as it stands and explain a possible purchase and its conditions. Tell us who is authorised to act and what the care arrangements mean for your timing. Your legal and financial advisers can help determine whether and when a sale fits the wider plan.

A house may contain possessions that still need decisions, or require work nobody in the family can take on. Explain the practical position rather than feel you must prepare everything first. A few things you may be wondering.

Can you advise how care should be funded?

No. Speak to the local authority and an independent adviser on care funding about your options. A cash offer is information to consider with that advice, not a recommendation to sell.

How will you assess my property?

We consider the property, its condition and any legal issues, then explain a written offer and its conditions. Ask us about anything unclear before deciding. There is no obligation to accept.

Are there fees or any obligation?

There is no charge to ask for an offer and no obligation to accept it. We charge no estate agent fees. We can cover legal fees if you use our panel solicitor; you can choose your own solicitor and pay their fees instead.

Can the offer change?

We base our offer on the property and the information available. It may change if legal checks reveal a material problem or the property differs from its description. We explain the offer and conditions in writing, and you can ask your solicitor to review them.
