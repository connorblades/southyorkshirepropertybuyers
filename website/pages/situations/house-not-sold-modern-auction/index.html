Your next step after modern auction

Where has your auction sale got stuck?

If your home hasn't sold through the modern method of auction (MMoA), you may have arranged photographs, answered questions and waited through another listing period. It's understandable to want a clear next step rather than another uncertain end date.

No acceptable bid

The auction ended without a buyer at a price you could accept. A further auction cycle is one option. You can also compare a direct cash offer, subject to your existing agreement.

A buyer pulled out

You thought a sale was moving forward, but it hasn't completed. Your solicitor can confirm whether a reservation still applies and when another sale can be agreed.

Another round of relisting

The property is still advertised and you want to consider a different route. An offer from us gives you a figure to compare without committing to switch.

Check your auction agreement before switching

An unsold property doesn't automatically mean the auction agreement has ended. Your solicitor should check the contract period, notice requirements, any direct-sale or withdrawal charges, and any buyer reservation still in place.

A sole selling rights agreement can restrict a different sale route or create a fee liability. We cannot cancel your auction agreement or remove charges owed to another company.

iamsold's seller FAQs explain its own arrangements. Your signed agreement is the one to check; auctioneers' terms differ.

A direct cash sale is a different route

A direct cash sale is a purchase by us, rather than another listing or auction. We aren't waiting for an onward sale or a buyer's mortgage application.

We assess each property individually, using its condition, location, tenure and the information you give us. We explain our offer in writing so you can consider it without obligation. It may change if legal checks reveal a material issue or the property differs from its description.

Compare the written terms for your property, including fees, ongoing costs, buyer funding and the proposed completion date. We assess each property individually. Your solicitor can explain the conditions, mortgage redemption and any other deductions before you decide.

SYPB charges no selling fee and covers the seller's conveyancing costs for a sale to us. Existing auction obligations and taxes are separate. We don't automatically cover amounts owed under another company's agreement.

Please note: taxes, including Capital Gains Tax and Stamp Duty Land Tax, are not covered by SYPB and remain the seller's responsibility where applicable. We recommend seeking independent tax advice if applicable.
