## Image audit, 2026-08-07

Every image in this folder was opened and described rather than trusted by its
filename. That is how the problem below went unnoticed for so long: the
filenames all read as UK property content, the pictures did not.

17 of 41 images were removed as unusable. Sixteen were shot outside
the UK and one was too blurred to use. Several actively contradicted the page
they sat on:

- five separate photographs of United States IRS Form 1040 tax returns, on UK
  capital gains, negative equity and mortgage redemption pages
- the Painted Ladies in San Francisco, on "how long does it take to sell a
  house UK"
- a blank US "LEASE" document with a Stars and Stripes keyring, on the England
  tenant rights page
- a plant that is not Japanese knotweed, on the Japanese knotweed page, where
  the reader is trying to identify the plant
- North American subdivisions with triple garages, on South Yorkshire town
  pages

Removed: best-cash-house-buyer-doncaster-2026.jpg, capital-gains-tax-buy-to-let-2026.jpg, cash-buyer-offer-vs-estate-agent-comparison.jpg, cash-buyer-scams-uk-2026.jpg, cash-buyer-vs-auction-uk-2026.jpg, house-on-market-too-long-what-to-do.jpg, how-long-does-it-take-to-sell-a-house-uk.jpg, is-it-worth-selling-to-a-house-buying-company.jpg, mortgage-redemption-solicitor-completion.jpg, negative-equity-options-mortgage-shortfall.jpg, probate-property-sale-executor-south-yorkshire.jpg, section-21-notice-tenant-rights-england.jpg, sell-house-without-estate-agent-uk.jpg, selling-house-divorce-uk.jpg, selling-house-japanese-knotweed-uk.jpg, selling-house-negative-equity-uk.jpg, tired-landlord-guide-2026.jpg
