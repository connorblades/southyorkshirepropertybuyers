About Us

South Yorkshire's local property buyers.

We are a small, experienced team of property investors based right here in South Yorkshire. We aren't a national call centre. When you contact us, you speak directly with the decision-maker, with no middlemen and no waiting on approvals from head office.

We have been buying residential and commercial property across Sheffield, Rotherham, Doncaster, Barnsley, and the surrounding areas for years. We know this market, we know the local solicitors, and we know how to move quickly when it matters.

We pay a fair cash price in exchange for speed, certainty, and zero hassle. Every property is different, so we price yours on what it actually is rather than on a formula. For many people in difficult circumstances, that trade-off is absolutely worth it, and we will always be honest with you about what to expect.

We can cover your legal fees if you use our panel solicitor, who knows exactly how we work, which keeps things moving quickly. You are free to use your own solicitor instead, and that is often worth doing, in which case their bill is yours. Either way, we charge you nothing.
