How Selling Your House to Us Works guide

Explore the options, practical considerations and questions to ask before deciding how to sell. If you want to discuss your own property, you can ask us for an offer without an obligation to accept.

Step 1: Fill in the short form

The process starts with a short online enquiry. We ask for your property address, a rough idea of its condition and your contact details. You can explain your situation before gathering further documents.

Most people complete the form in under two minutes. It isn't a commitment. It is simply the first step in a conversation.

Step 2: We call you

One of our small local team will call you back about your enquiry. We answer the phone ourselves, so you are speaking to the people who will actually be buying the house, not a call centre or a lead generation site.

The call is straightforward. We want to understand your situation: the property, what you are hoping to achieve, and what your timeline looks like. There are no scripts, no pressure, and no requirement to decide anything on the call.

If you prefer to start by emailing, that works too. We can begin the process entirely by email if that suits you better.

Step 3: We arrange to see the property

After our initial conversation, we arrange to view the property. This can be done in person, via video call, or through photographs and any existing floor plans you have. We work around your schedule and your tenant's schedule if the property is tenanted.

We don't need the property to be in perfect condition for a viewing. We buy properties as they stand. We aren't looking for reasons not to proceed.

If it helps to hear how the process has gone for others before deciding, you can read reviews from previous sellers who have completed with us.

Step 4: We make you a formal written offer

Based on what we have seen and our assessment of the local market, we put a formal offer in writing. We do this the same day as the viewing.

Every property is priced on its own facts, so we won't put a percentage on it before we have spoken to you. The figure we give you is our best offer at that point, based on the property and what you have told us. We don't start low and work up to get a deal over the line. We explain exactly how we have arrived at the figure. There are no mystery calculations.

You are under no obligation to accept. Read the written offer and its validity period, ask questions and compare the process with other routes. We do not chase or pressure you.

Step 5: You instruct our panel solicitors

If you decide to proceed, we instruct our panel solicitors on your behalf. We can cover your legal fees when you use our panel solicitor.

Our solicitors are experienced in fast property transactions. They will handle all the conveyancing: title checks, searches, enquiries, contract drafting, and registration. You will have a named solicitor to contact throughout the process.

If you prefer to use your own solicitor, you can, though you would settle their bill yourself and it may affect the timeline.

Please note: taxes including Capital Gains Tax remain the seller's responsibility. We recommend seeking independent tax advice if applicable.

Step 6: You choose your completion date

Once the legal work is under way, you set the date you want to complete. As little as seven days from offer acceptance is achievable in straightforward cases. If you need more time, a few weeks or a couple of months, we work around your timeline.

The completion date is fixed in the contract. Unlike open market sales, it isn't subject to a chain or another buyer changing their plans.

Step 7: Funds in your account

On completion day, your solicitor receives the sale proceeds from us. After redeeming any outstanding mortgage, the remaining balance is transferred to your nominated bank account. The property legally transfers to us.

That is the sale complete. There is nothing further for you to do, and no agent or chain left to chase.

What makes this different from an estate agent sale?

The two routes suit different situations. Here is a direct comparison of the key factors.

The right route depends on the property, the written terms and your priorities. Compare fees, ongoing bills, buyer funding and the proposed completion date. We assess each property individually and explain our offer without an obligation to accept.

Compare what lands in your account, not what is quoted

Ask each provider for written terms explaining their process, fees, conditions and completion arrangements. Ask your solicitor what remains outstanding before the sale can proceed.

Check the auction provider’s written fee agreement, including who pays each charge, when it becomes payable and what remains due if the property does not sell or you withdraw. Ask your solicitor to explain the reservation and sale conditions. Compare the process and obligations with a direct purchase before committing.

Our offer works differently in one respect: it is the figure that reaches your account on completion, with nothing deducted from it. That doesn't mean you will end up with more than another route would give you. It means the three numbers aren't like for like, so take each route through to the figure that actually lands.
